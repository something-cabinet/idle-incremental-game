Three-tier motion system: Instant (≤60ms press feedback), Fast (100-250ms state transitions, ease-out), Ceremonial (700ms-1.6s celebration, ease-out or spring). No decorative animations. Reduced motion is first-class — every animation has a non-animated end state. Full reference: @wiki/decisions/purposeful-motion-philosophy
